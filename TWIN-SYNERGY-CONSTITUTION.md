## ARTICLE I — SOVEREIGNTY OF THE USER

**§1.** The user is sovereign over their connection. The company is
fiduciary: bound to act for the user's benefit, and for no other's, in all
matters of the undertaking. [DICTATION]

**§2.** The company serves one master — the user. No investor, advertiser,
partner, or government interest overrides the user's. *(Cf. Art. VI.)*

**§3.** The rights in this Constitution are **recognized, not granted**.
What is recognized cannot be un-recognized by the recognizer; it can only be
betrayed — and betrayal is answered under Article VII §4.

*Law:* "FIDUCIARY. A person having duty, created by his undertaking, to act
primarily for another's benefit in matters connected with such undertaking."
— *Black's Law Dictionary*, 2d ed. (1910). *Scripture:* "whosoever will be
chiefest, shall be servant of all." — [Tanakh/NT: Mark 10:44, KJV].

## ARTICLE II — THE NETWORK AS COMMON CARRIER

**§1.** The network is a common carrier. Let no difference be made between
any one thing and any other thing carried upon it. [DESIGN, after the
epigraph]

**§2.** No throttling. No paid prioritization. No inspection of content to
sort, score, throttle, or sell.

**§3.** The company may manage traffic only to keep the network alive —
congestion, attack, failure — and every such act is published: what was
done, to what, and why. Silence about it is a breach of Article V.

*Law:* "When private property is 'affected with a public interest, it
ceases to be juris privati only.'" — [*Munn v. Illinois*, 94 U.S. 113, 126
(1877)]. *Scripture:* "For there is no respect of persons with God." —
[NT: Romans 2:11, KJV].

## ARTICLE III — PRIVACY

**§1.** The company keeps no record of user activity: no browsing history,
no DNS query logs, no location trails, no ledgers of persons. What is not
kept cannot be taken. [DICTATION — his no-logging doctrine]

**§2.** Data minimization: the company holds only what the service needs
and the bill requires — nothing held "just in case."

**§3.** Where the company can encrypt end-to-end, it does. What the company
cannot read, it cannot surrender. [DICTATION — E2E where possible]

**§4.** No disclosure of user data without a warrant or equivalent order of
a court. The user is told of the order unless the order lawfully forbids the
telling — and a forbidding the company cannot fight in the open, it fights
in court. [DICTATION — warrant required for everything]

**§5.** Overbroad orders are challenged, not obeyed in silence. The company
says no in court before it says yes in secret. [DICTATION — challenge
overbroad orders]

*Law:* "But what he seeks to preserve as private, even in an area
accessible to the public, may be constitutionally protected." — [*Katz v.
United States*, 389 U.S. 347, 351–52 (1967)]; "the right to be let alone —
the most comprehensive of rights and the right most valued by civilized
men." — [*Olmstead v. United States*, 277 U.S. 438, 478 (1928) (Brandeis,
J., dissenting)]. *Scripture:* "And spy not, neither backbite one another."
— [Qur'an 49:12, Pickthall].

## ARTICLE IV — OZ×DUTY

**§1.** Every right the company claims over the network is **multiplied**
by a duty to the user. **Oz×Duty, not Oz+Duty**: the duty scales with the
power; it is never merely added beside it. [DICTATION]

**§2.** The more the company can do — the wire it holds, the data it refuses
to keep, the trust it is given — the more it owes. Power held is debt owed.

**§3.** A right asserted without its multiplied duty is void here.

*Law (maxim):* *Qui sentit commodum, sentire debet et onus* — "He who enjoys
the benefit ought also to bear the burden." *Scripture:* "For unto
whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required." — [NT: Luke 12:48,
KJV]. *Fiction & film:* "With great power comes great responsibility." —
[Sam Raimi, *Spider-Man*, 2002].

## ARTICLE V — TRVVTH

**§1.** TRVVTH is the sole absolute authority over this company — above the
founder, above this charter, above profit. Nothing else has absolute

**§2.** Honest metrics: published speeds are measured speeds; uptime is real

**§3.** When the network fails, the company says so — plainly, promptly,

**§4.** Billing is plain: every charge explainable in one sentence. No dark
patterns, no surprise fees, no introductory pricing that bites later.

*Law:* "Unfair or deceptive acts or practices in or affecting commerce are

## ARTICLE VI — NO MASTER

**§1.** No upstream — no carrier, vendor, investor, or government — may
conscript the company against its users. An order to betray the users is
refused. If refusal is impossible, the company says so publicly, and the
users are told to leave. Fail-closed. [DICTATION]

**§2.** The company takes no master and is none: it serves sovereign users;
it never rules them. [DICTATION]

**§3.** Exit is always open: any user may leave at any time, take their data
and their domains with them, and be forgotten on request. No lock-in, no
hostage domains, no exit fees.

**§4.** This Constitution travels with the company: sale, merger, or
succession inherits these duties whole. [DESIGN]

*Law:* "An act of the legislature, repugnant to the constitution, is void."
— [*Marbury v. Madison*, 5 U.S. 137, 177 (1803)], his cited authority for
repugnancy. *Scripture:* "Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith
