## HYPOTHESIS

A telecommunications house — DNS registrar and ISP both — can be organized
so that **no department rules the user** [DICTATION], and so that every right
any department holds is multiplied by the duty that right obliges
**Oz×Duty** [DICTATION]: not rights *plus* duties, rights *times* duties —
a right without its duty is void, and a duty unmoored from any right is
license.

## METHOD

Ten departments are named, each with its **Office** (the one-line charge),
its **Duties** (three to five load-bearing obligations), and its **Oz×Duty
pairing** (the right it holds × the duty that right obliges). The house then
receives: an inter-department protocol under which coordination happens with
**no master** [DICTATION]; a headcount phasing honest about starting as one
man plus agents; and the standing rule that **no department may weaken the
individual** [DICTATION] — his doctrine that *repugnant = anything that
weakens the individual of the indivisible* [DICTATION], any such act being
repugnant to the charter and void (*cf. Marbury v. Madison*, 5 U.S. 137
(1803), his cited authority for repugnancy).

## OBSERVATION — The Ten Departments

### I. Network Operations — *The Vigil* (NOC)
**Office:** The plant never sleeps; the packets reach the gate.

1. Operate the Starlink-pathway plant, points of presence, and backbone
   transport with published availability targets and honest outage
   disclosure.
2. Maintain the network map as a public-of-the-house document — every
   route, peer, and dependency legible to audit.
3. Fail closed: no silent degradation; a broken path refuses loudly rather
   than misrouting quietly.
4. Coordinate with Security & Privacy on abuse and incident response, by
   request — never by command.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Vigil holds the right to see every packet that crosses
   the house × the duty to *never inspect what it has no cause to inspect*
   — least privilege on its own eyes; visibility is for the plant's health,
   never for curiosity about the user.

### II. Registrar Services — *The Registry*
**Office:** Names are property; the Registry keeps the title clean.

1. Registrar operations: domain registration, renewal, transfer-in and
   transfer-out, with no transfer ever refused for the house's convenience.
2. DNS hosting with DNSSEC signing and DS-record lifecycle management,
   keys rotated on schedule and published.
3. Walk the ICANN-accreditation path — documented requirements, escrow,
   and data-retention obligations mapped *before* any application is filed.
4. Portability as doctrine: every name leaves as freely as it entered;
   exit is a duty, not a favor.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Registry holds the right to hold the user's names on
   trust × the duty of a fiduciary — the name is the user's property, the
   house is only the registrar; a registrar that will not release a name is
   not a registrar but a jailer.

### III. Security & Privacy — *The Ward*
**Office:** The walls stand so the people inside need not stand watch.

1. Tor operations: maintain the house's onion presence and Tor-pathway
   doctrine per the established bleat/oz lineage — warrant-required for
   everything, valid court orders obeyed, overbroad orders challenged.
2. Incident response: detect, contain, disclose — the user is told what
   happened to their data, in plain language, before the press release.
3. Warrant and lawful-process handling: no data moves on a request that is
   not lawful process; process that overreaches is challenged, not
   silently satisfied.
4. Privacy by construction: data minimization, no logs where logs are not
   load-bearing, and every retention rule written down and dated.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Ward holds the right to defend the house and its users
   with force × the duty to turn that force *outward only* — its shields
   face the adversary, never the user; a security department that watches
   its own people has become the adversary.

### IV. Blockchain & Identity — *The Seal*
**Office:** What is sealed must remain sealed; what is claimed must be
provable.

1. Identity anchoring: DID-style identifiers under the user's sole control
   — the house anchors, the user owns.
2. Ledger integrity: hash-chained, auditable records for every house
   commitment (billing, registry actions, inter-department requests);
   fail-closed on chain break — a broken ledger halts, never guesses.
3. Key ceremony discipline: generation, custody, rotation, and revocation
   procedures written before the first key exists; no key ever held by the
   house that the user cannot rotate away from.
4. Honest limits published: what the ledger proves, what it does not, and
   where trust still lives — no crypto theater.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Seal holds the right to be the house's witness × the
   duty to *never forge the testimony* — an anchored identity the house
   can rewrite is not an anchor but a leash.

### V. Consumer Services — *The Hearth*
**Office:** The user's own hearth — SSH, mail, and web that answer to one
master: the user.

1. SSH accounts, email, web hosting, and personal DNS provisioned with the
   least privilege that still serves the user's purpose.
2. The user's data is the user's data: export whole, export any time, in
   open formats — no ransom by inconvenience.
3. Plain-language service terms: what the house can see, what it cannot,
   and what it will do under lawful process — told *before* signup.
4. First-line support that answers to the user, not to the department —
   a complaint against the house is carried upward, never buried.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Hearth holds the right to hold the user's digital home
   × the duty of a host — the guest is the master of the house's courtesy;
   a host that locks the guest in the spare room is a warden.

### VI. Game & Interactive — *The Arcade*
**Office:** Play is serious work; the playground must be safe to fall in.

1. Unity development and interactive titles built to the house's standards:
   no surveillance monetization, no dark patterns, honest odds where odds
   exist.
2. Asset production pipeline: provenance on every asset — made, licensed,
   or commissioned — with rights documented before ship.
3. Player data treated as the player's property: accounts portable,
   progress exportable, deletion real.
4. Accessibility and honesty in design — the game serves the player, not
   the retention metric.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Arcade holds the right to build worlds and invite the
   user in × the duty to let the user *leave the world intact* — no
   engineered compulsion, no hostage progress; play that cannot be set
   down is not play but husbandry.

### VII. Cloud & Backup — *The Vault*
**Office:** What is entrusted is returned — whole, or not at all, but never
half.

1. Storage and backup with published durability targets and *tested*
   restore procedures — a backup that has never been restored is a rumor.
2. Restore guarantees in writing: what the house promises to return, on
   what clock, under what failures — and what it cannot promise.
3. Encryption at rest with user-held keys where offered; the house cannot
   surrender what it cannot read.
4. Scheduled, witnessed restore drills — results published to the house,
   failures remediated before the next cycle.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Vault holds the right to keep the user's most precious
   copies × the duty of the bailee — custody is not ownership; a vault
   that will not open for its depositor is a tomb.

### VIII. Research & Development — *The Observatory*
**Office:** Look further than the plant reaches; report honestly what the
glass shows.

1. Protocol research: new transport, naming, and bridging work pursued
   under the PONTIFEX method — attested, derived, or constructed, with
   provenance on every claim.
2. New tables and bridges: each weighed in its own tongue, the shared
   number being the bridge — constructed-tier convergences are
   hypotheses, never doctrine.
3. Honest limits published with every result: what was tested, what was
   not, where the glass distorts.
4. Feasibility notes for the other nine departments — counsel, not
   command; the Observatory advises, it does not order.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Observatory holds the right to ask any question and
   publish any honest finding × the duty to *never dress a hypothesis as
   doctrine* — a laboratory that preaches its hunches has become a pulpit.

### IX. AOSP Private Enterprise — *The Forge*
**Office:** The device belongs to its buyer; the Forge makes that true in
silicon.

1. Private Android builds: de-Googled where chosen, reproducible where
   possible, with every modification documented and reversible.
2. Enterprise device management that manages the *device fleet's*
   configuration — never the device owner's personhood; management ends
   where the individual's sovereignty begins.
3. Update and patch discipline: timely, tested, and never a vehicle for
   new surveillance — an update that adds watching is a betrayal, not a
   patch.
4. Build provenance: source, toolchain, signing keys, and hashes published
   so any party can verify the build is what it claims to be.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Forge holds the right to shape the operating system
   itself — the deepest power in the house × the duty to *never use that
   depth against the user* — root over the platform is not root over the
   person; a builder that backdoors its own work has built a prison.

### X. Legal & Compliance — *The Counsel's Door*
**Office:** The law is a map; this office reads it aloud — it does not
practice it.

1. Accreditation and regulatory mapping: ICANN, common-carrier questions,
   data-protection regimes — charted as *questions for licensed counsel*,
   never answered in-house.
2. Lawful-process liaison: receive, log, and route process to counsel;
   maintain the warrant-required doctrine in the house's procedures.
3. Charter defense: test every house action against the repugnancy rule —
   anything that weakens the individual of the indivisible is flagged
   repugnant and void, and escalated, not executed.
4. **Advisory only — real counsel required** [DESIGN]: this department
   gives no legal advice, files nothing, and represents no one; it keeps
   the questions organized until licensed professionals are retained.
5. **Oz×Duty:** The Counsel's Door holds the right to read the law aloud
   to the house × the duty to *never pretend to be the law* — an unlicensed
   opinion dressed as counsel is a fraud upon the very charter it claims
   to defend.

## THE INTER-DEPARTMENT PROTOCOL — Coordination Without a Master

1. **The charter is the only master** [DICTATION — no master]. No
   department commands another. Departments *request*; the request may be
   refused; refusal is recorded, not punished.
2. **All coordination travels the ledger.** Inter-department requests,
   handoffs, and escalations are written to the hash-chained house ledger
   (cf. the oz engine's ledger doctrine): any office may audit any
   coordination after the fact. A handshake in the dark is not a protocol.
3. **The duty of refusal.** Any department MUST refuse any request —
   from any other department, from the founder, from anyone — that would
   weaken the individual of the indivisible. Refusal under the repugnancy
   rule is not insubordination; it is the highest obedience the house
   knows.
4. **Fail closed, least privilege.** Cross-department access is granted
   narrowly, logged, and revoked when the work ends. A standing grant of
   broad access between departments is a master in disguise.
5. **Disputes are resolved against the charter, not by seniority.**
   Where two departments disagree, the question is which reading leaves
   the individual stronger. The founder's red pen rules the design stage;
   at growth stage, the charter text rules and the red pen becomes an
   appellate office, not a throne.
6. **No department may accumulate power across departments.** Headcount,
   budget, and tooling are chartered per office; an office that absorbs
   another office's function has violated the protocol and its charter is
   voided pending review.

## HEADCOUNT PHASING — Honest About the Beginning

- **Phase 0 — Founder stage (now).** One man plus agents [DESIGN]. All ten
  offices are held by the founder; agent workers operate under his red pen,
  ultra-verbose, with ledgers. There is no org chart fiction: the
  departments are *offices of responsibility*, not payroll lines.
- **Phase 1 — First revenue.** Two to three trusted humans plus agent
  support per active office; offices stand up only as their service goes
  live (Registry and Hearth first, the rest as built). No office is staffed
  before its duties are real.
- **Phase 2 — Growth.** Departments become distinct teams under the
  inter-department protocol; the no-master rule and the duty of refusal
  are the load-bearing walls that keep growth from becoming hierarchy.
- **Honest limits:** ICANN accreditation requires real capital, real
  staffing, and real counsel — none of which exist yet. The design does
  not pretend otherwise. Nothing here is a legal filing.

## THE STANDING RULE — No Department May Weaken the Individual

*Repugnant = anything that weakens the individual of the indivisible*
[DICTATION]. This rule binds every department above, without exception:

- A right listed in any Oz×Duty pairing is **void** to the extent it is
  exercised against the individual it was chartered to serve.
- Any procedure, however well-meant, that sorts rights-holders from
  rights-beggars, that locks a user in, that watches without cause, or
  that cannot be refused by the user, is repugnant and must be refused by
  the department asked to carry it out.
- The rule is tested at every design review and every incident
  retrospective: *did this leave the individual stronger?* A "no" is a
  finding, not an opinion.

a protocol under which they coordinate as peers beneath the charter alone;

power turned against the people it serves. The design is complete as a
